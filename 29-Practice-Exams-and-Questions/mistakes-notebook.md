# Mistakes Notebook

## Template per question

- Domain:
- Topic:
- Option chosen:
- Correct option:
- Technical reason for the mistake:
- Decision rule to avoid repeating it:

## Mistake classification

- **Concept:** confusion between services/patterns.
- **Reading:** ignored a key requirement in the question.
- **Trade-off:** chose technically correct options that were worse on cost/operations.
- **Rushing:** answered without eliminating options.

## Weekly routine (20 minutes)

1. Group mistakes by recurring topic.
2. Reread only `cheatsheet.md` and `questions.md` from the affected module.
3. Redo 5 questions focused on the same type of mistake.
4. Update the objective decision rule.

## Progress indicator

- High recurrence: the same mistake in 2 or more practice exams.
- Controlled recurrence: mistake fixed within 1 review cycle.
- Consolidated domain: consistent score above 80% on the topic.
