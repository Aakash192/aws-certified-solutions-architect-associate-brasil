# Review Flashcards

## Card 01

**Question:** What should you do before opening the answer to a question?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Identify the dominant requirement, eliminate clearly incompatible options and only then compare trade-offs.

</details>

## Card 02

**Question:** How do you use the mistakes notebook without wasting time?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Record the service you confused, the signal you ignored in the question and the practical rule to avoid repeating the mistake.

</details>

## Card 03

**Question:** What is the main sign of a trick in cost questions?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Choosing the cheapest option without validating availability, latency, minimum retention or recurring operation.

</details>

## Card 04

**Question:** When does a managed option tend to be better?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
When the question asks for low operation, automatic scaling, managed resilience or less maintenance effort.

</details>

## Card 05

**Question:** What separates a correct answer from a merely possible one?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
The correct one meets the explicit requirement with less operational risk and without unnecessary components.

</details>

## Card 06

**Question:** How do you review by domain in the final stretch?

<details>
<summary><strong>Show answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Group your mistakes by resilience, performance, security and cost, then redo only the most recurring points.

</details>
